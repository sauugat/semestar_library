About Semester Library

Gandaki University Academic Resources

What is Semester Library?

Semester Library is a dedicated academic resource platform built specifically for Gandaki University students. It serves as a centralized hub where students can access comprehensive study materials structured around their academic curriculum.

Who can use Semester Library?

While the platform's detailed notes, class streams, and collaborative features are exclusively available to authenticated Gandaki University students, the core academic information—such as the BIT syllabus, examination routines, and official university notices—is publicly accessible to everyone.

What academic resources are available?

Students can access a wide range of resources including:

• Semester Notes: Detailed, crowdsourced notes and cheat sheets for every subject.
• Syllabus: The complete 8-semester curriculum breakdown, including unit-wise topics and credit details.
• Exam Routines: Up-to-date schedules for upcoming semester examinations.
• University Notices: Important academic circulars and announcements.

Where can students find the syllabus and routines?

The Syllabus section contains the complete structured curriculum for all semesters. The Exam Routines section lists the examination schedules and dates. Both are available without logging in.
