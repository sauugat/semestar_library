How to Install in 3 Easy Steps

Because this APK is distributed directly to university students, your browser will ask for quick confirmation. Follow these 3 simple steps:

1

Download the .APK

Tap the Download for Android button or scan the QR code above from your Android phone's camera.

💡 File size is ~112 MB. Download over Wi-Fi or mobile data.
2

Tap "Download Anyway"

If Chrome or your browser warns: "File might be harmful", tap Download anyway.

🛡️ This is Android's standard notice for apps installed directly outside the Play Store.
3

Open & Install

Tap the downloaded file in your notification drawer or Downloads folder and select Install.

⚙️ If prompted for "Unknown Sources", tap Settings and toggle Allow from this source.
