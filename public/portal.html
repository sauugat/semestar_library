ALPINE RESEARCH ANNAPURNA BASIN

Annapurna Himalayan Medicinal Flora Research

Situated beneath Mt. Machhapuchhre, GU's Faculty of Pharmacy conducts direct clinical isolation on rare Himalayan herbs like Yarsagumba (Cordyceps) and endemic alpine medicinal flora.

GOVERNANCE ESTD. 2019

Nepal's 1st Autonomous Provincial University

Founded under Gandaki Provincial Assembly Act 2076, Gandaki University is Nepal's first university governed by an independent Board of Trustees (BOT) rather than centralized ministries.
