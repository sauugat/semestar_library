Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Application: Semester Library · Effective Date: October 2, 2026 · Version: 1.0.0
Core Commitment: Semester Library is an academic resource and collaboration tool designed exclusively for university students and faculty. We do not sell user data, we do not employ third-party advertising trackers, and we restrict collection strictly to features necessary for academic operations and reliable notification delivery.

1. Identification & Institutional Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, and protection of information by Semester Library, an academic portal developed for Gandaki University students, faculty, and administrative staff ("we", "us", or "the Platform"). This policy applies to the Semester Library mobile application for Android and iOS as well as the affiliated web portal hosted at https://semestar-library.vercel.app.

2. Categories of Information We Collect

To provide curriculum resources, classroom communications, and authenticated services, we collect the following categories of data:

A. Student & Faculty Identification

  • Student ID / Roll Number: Used as the primary academic identifier for login, semester placement, and course verification.
  • Full Name & Email Address: Used for account identity, official communication, and password recovery.
  • Username & Gender: Used for in-app profile presentation.
  • Password Hash: Stored using salted one-way cryptographic hashing (bcrypt) to verify login credentials securely. Plaintext passwords are never logged or stored.
  • Profile Picture & Bio (Optional): Uploaded by the user to personalize their classmate profile.

B. Academic & Course Placement Metadata

  • Academic Program: E.g., Bachelor of Information Technology (BIT).
  • Current Semester: E.g., Semester 1 through Semester 8, used to filter subject curricula, notices, and relevant study notes.
  • Role Designation: Student, Class Representative (CR), Teacher, or Administrator.

C. User-Generated Educational Content

  • Study Materials: PDF notes, presentation slides, syllabus documents, and past examination papers uploaded by teachers and students.
  • Feed Posts & Announcements: Status updates, official administrative notices, assignment briefs, comments, and post likes.
  • Realtime Group Chat Messages: Group messages and attachments shared within official departmental semester chats.

D. Device Telemetry & Push Notification Identifiers

  • Expo Push Token & FCM Registration Token: Unique device identifiers required to route real-time push notifications for chat messages, official circulars, and study notes.
  • Device Hardware & OS Attributes: Device model (e.g., Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel), operating system platform (Android / iOS), and app version, utilized solely for push channel routing and layout compatibility.
  • Last Active Timestamp: Recorded to prune obsolete notification tokens for uninstalled or inactive devices.

Zero Advertising Policy: Semester Library contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no ad personalization trackers (such as Google AdMob or Meta Audience Network), and does not participate in programmatic data broker exchanges.

4. In-App and Public Account & Data Deletion

In full compliance with Google Play Developer Policy and international data privacy principles, users possess the absolute right to delete their account and associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion:

  1. In-App Self-Service: Open Settings → Navigate to Account → Tap Delete Account → Enter your current password to confirm permanent erasure.
  2. Public Web Deletion Portal (No App Required): If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your mobile device, visit our public web portal at https://semestar-library.vercel.app/delete-account to securely authenticate and delete your account.
  3. Direct Privacy Officer Assistance: Send an email from your registered address to privacy@gandakiuniversity.edu.np requesting account deletion.
Scope of Immediate Erasure:
Upon deletion, your student credentials (name, email, password hash, bio, avatar), mobile bearer authentication tokens, registered device push tokens, notification preferences, personal likes, comments, and private social relationships are immediately and permanently purged from active databases and session stores.

Data Retention & Deletion Schedule

Data Category Post-Deletion Treatment Legal & Operational Rationale
Account Credentials & Profile (Name, Email, Student ID, Password Hash, Bio, Avatar) DELETED Immediate, irreversible cryptographic purge from active databases.
Active Sessions & Bearer Tokens DELETED All mobile authentication tokens and server session cookies are revoked instantly.
Push Tokens & Hardware Attributes DELETED Device tokens unregistered from Expo & FCM; outbox queue purged.
Notification Preferences DELETED Granular preference records wiped immediately.
Feed Posts & Post Images DELETED Personal status updates and attached photos are purged from database and file storage.
Comments (Feed Comments & Material Comments) DELETED All personal comments authored by the student are purged permanently.
Social Interactions (Likes & Follows) DELETED Personal likes and social graph relationships are removed entirely.
Realtime Group Chat Messages ANONYMIZED / DECOUPLED Classroom discussion messages remain in group history to prevent disruption of ongoing student conversations, but the sender record is purged so messages cannot be linked to user identity.
Uploaded Academic Course Materials (PDF Notes, Past Papers) ANONYMIZED / DECOUPLED Publicly contributed educational materials in departmental folders are retained under the Academic Continuity Exception for enrolled students, with author association severed.

Academic Continuity Exception: Publicly shared academic course materials (e.g. past question papers or syllabus notes) contributed to departmental folders are decoupled from your personal identity and retained as anonymous academic records to preserve educational access for enrolled university students.

5. Notification Controls & User Preferences

Students retain full granular control over notification behavior. Inside the mobile application (Settings → Notification Preferences), you may independently toggle:

  • Mute Group Chat notifications
  • Study Material & Past Question alerts
  • Classmate Discussion & Post alerts
  • Official Administration Notices & Circulars
  • Hide Notification Previews on Lock Screen (masks message bodies for privacy)

6. Data Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical safeguards to protect student data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

  • All client-to-server traffic is strictly transmitted over HTTPS / TLS 1.3.
  • Database queries employ strict parameterized bindings to eliminate SQL injection vulnerabilities.
  • Administrative and notice publication privileges are strictly enforced server-side based on authenticated role tokens.
